AI-Powered Customer Support Automation

Intelligent Email Triage, AI Response Drafting & Human Review

n8nGmailOpenAIAirtableSlack

An n8n workflow that processes incoming emails, checks for duplicates, classifies inquiries, prepares response drafts, tracks requests in Airtable, and notifies the team in Slack. Every customer reply remains a human decision.

01 / THE PROJECT

From inbox overload
to guided action.

Routine support work can be structured and accelerated without giving up control of what customers receive.

THE CHALLENGE

Every message starts from scratch.

Support teams read incoming emails, identify request types, check for existing requests, prepare replies, and decide what needs attention. Repeating that work can slow operations and make tracking harder.

THE APPROACH

Automate preparation.
Keep people in control.

I connected Gmail, OpenAI, Airtable, and Slack through n8n to organize new requests and prepare suggested replies. The workflow creates Gmail drafts for review; it does not send customer emails.

03 / WALKTHROUGH

From new email
to reviewable draft.

Six stages show how the automation moves a customer inquiry through processing, tracking, and internal notification.

01 / INTAKE

Email intake & preparation

Gmail detects a new customer email. JavaScript extracts and organizes the message details for the rest of the workflow.

GMAIL TRIGGER → JAVASCRIPT
02 / FILTER

Duplicate prevention

Airtable searches for an existing message record. The “Is New Message?” decision lets new inquiries continue; duplicates end at that branch.

AIRTABLE SEARCH → DECISION
03 / INTELLIGENCE

AI classification & response

OpenAI classifies the request, drafts a suggested reply, returns a confidence score, and marks requests that need review. The structured output is passed to validation.

BillingAccessSchedulingTechnical SupportGeneral QuestionRefund Request
04 / VALIDATION

Validation & tracking

JavaScript checks required AI output before the request is saved to Airtable. Invalid output is handled by the validation logic; Airtable provides the request record used for tracking.

JAVASCRIPT → AIRTABLE
05 / DRAFT

Gmail draft generation

The workflow creates a suggested reply as a Gmail draft for a person to inspect and send. Draft creation occurs before the review decision and either Slack notification.

GMAIL DRAFT → HUMAN REVIEW
01 / 03
06 / NOTIFY

Slack notifications & escalation

After draft creation, the review decision routes one of two internal Slack messages: a draft-ready notice or an alert for requests needing human attention. Customer email sending stays manual.

DRAFT READY / NEEDS REVIEW
04 / HUMAN OVERSIGHT

Built to assist.
Designed for review.

The automation prepares useful information for support staff while keeping outbound communication under human control.
